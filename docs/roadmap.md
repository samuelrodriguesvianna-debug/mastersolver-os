# MasterSolver-OS – Product & Technical Roadmap

This roadmap describes the planned evolution of MasterSolver-OS
from MVP to a full governance and decision intelligence platform.

---

## Phase 0 – MVP (Current)

**Status:** ✅ Completed

Focus:
- Cloud-native FastAPI service
- Health and status endpoints
- Containerized deployment (Cloud Run)
- Architectural foundation

Version:
- v0.1.0-mvp

---

## Phase 1 – Signal Foundation

**Goal:** Capture and normalize weak operational signals.

Planned features:
- Signal ingestion endpoints
- Signal classification and tagging
- Basic signal persistence
- Time-based signal analysis

Expected outcome:
- Visibility into early operational anomalies

---

## Phase 2 – Risk Intelligence

**Goal:** Transform signals into measurable risk.

Planned features:
- Risk scoring engine
- Threshold-based alerts
- Correlation between signals
- Risk trend visualization (API-level)

Expected outcome:
- Quantified, explainable risk indicators

---

## Phase 3 – Decision Readiness

**Goal:** Support high-stakes decisions with context.

Planned features:
- Decision readiness index
- Context aggregation (signals + risk)
- Decision traceability
- Scenario comparison

Expected outcome:
- Faster, safer and more transparent decisions

---

## Phase 4 – Governance Layer

**Goal:** Enable enterprise-grade governance.

Planned features:
- Multi-tenant support
- Role-based access control
- Governance policies
- Audit and compliance trails

Expected outcome:
- Trust and accountability at scale

---

## Phase 5 – AI & Agent Intelligence

**Goal:** Simulate and assist decision-making.

Planned features:
- LLM-assisted analysis
- Agent-based simulations
- Predictive risk modeling
- What-if scenario engines

Expected outcome:
- Proactive, AI-assisted governance

---

## Guiding Principle

MasterSolver-OS will evolve incrementally,
without sacrificing clarity, explainability or control.
